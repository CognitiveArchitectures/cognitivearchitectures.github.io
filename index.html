What we care about

Private by default

Your data is yours. It lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, works without a connection, and is never sold or used to track you.

Preserve what matters

Your knowledge and your data outlive any app, kept in open formats you can always take with you.

Let the rest go

Not everything is worth keeping. What matters survives, and memory brings it back. We build against hoarding, not for it.

Accessible and connected

Knowledge is only worth keeping if you can reach it, and see how it links to the rest.

Start, then refine

Perfectionism stalls. Begin, use it, and improve along the way: calm tools that leave room for the work.
